# Cryptonite: Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** *the stone is a candlestick.* A Kryptonite crystal shaped as a trading candle, with the price wick running straight through the stone. Outside the crystal the wick is a solid line; inside it is a cut that splits the stone into a lit facet and a shadow facet.
- **Wordmark:** custom-drawn capitals. Every curve is cut at the crystal's 45°, so the type and the symbol share one geometry. Never retype the name in a font: always use the files.
- **Versions** (all in `svg/`):

| Version | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | `cryptonite-horizontal-*.svg` | Headers, documents, most uses |
| Stacked | `cryptonite-stacked-*.svg` | Square or narrow spaces, splash screens |
| Symbol | `cryptonite-symbol-*.svg` | Avatars and icons from 48 px up |
| Symbol, small cut | `cryptonite-symbol-small-*.svg` | Anything under 48 px: wider wick and broader stone |
| Wordmark only | `cryptonite-wordmark-*.svg` | Only where the symbol already appears nearby |
| Favicon / app icon | `apps/web/src/app/` (`favicon.ico`, `icon.svg`, `apple-icon.png`) and `apps/web/public/icon-*.png` | Browser tabs, PWA, home screen |

Colour schemes in every version:
- `colour`: on white or light backgrounds.
- `reversed`: on Obsidian or other dark backgrounds. The wicks and type are in Quartz, and the strokes are slightly thinner to make up for light spreading on dark.
- `black`, `white`: one colour.
- `mono`: one colour in Facet green.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × F** on every side, where **F** is the width of one crystal facet (about ¼ of the symbol's height). F is also the gap between the symbol and the wordmark in the horizontal lockup. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 48 px tall (below that use the small cut) | 10 mm |
| Small cut | 16 px | 5 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kryptonite | lit facet | `#7CDB4C` | 124 219 76 | 50 0 90 0 | 7488 C |
| Facet | shadow facet, mono colour (the app's `--primary`) | `#419547` | 65 149 71 | 75 10 90 5 | 7740 C |
| Ore | wicks and type on light | `#0F2B1C` | 15 43 28 | 80 40 75 75 | Black 3 C |
| Obsidian | dark background, app-icon tile | `#0A120E` | 10 18 14 | 75 55 65 85 | Black 6 C |
| Quartz | wicks and type on dark | `#EAF7E3` | 234 247 227 | 7 0 10 0 | paper / 7485 C tint |

The CMYK and Pantone values are starting points. Check them against a physical swatch book before printing.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- `colour` on white, Quartz or light grey.
- `reversed` on Obsidian or near-black.
- `white` on Facet green.
- `black` on white.

**Don't use these pairs:**
- `colour` on Facet green: the shadow facet disappears.
- `colour` on black: the Ore wicks disappear.

**Accessibility:** Kryptonite green is only 1.7:1 against white, so never use it for text or thin lines on light backgrounds. Ore and Facet are fine for text: Ore is 15:1 on white, and Facet is 3.7:1, which is enough for large text only.

## 5. Typography
- The wordmark is custom outlines, so there is no font licence to manage.
- Interface and documents: **Inter** (SIL Open Font License), weights 400, 600 and 700. Use SemiBold capitals with about 8% letter-spacing for headings that sit near the logo.
- Code and numbers: **JetBrains Mono** (OFL).

## 6. Don'ts
Don't:
- stretch, squash or rotate the logo;
- swap the facet colours (light always comes from the left);
- remove the wick or close the cut through the stone;
- add glows, gradients, shadows or outlines (no neon "radioactive" glow);
- change the size or spacing between the symbol and the wordmark;
- retype the wordmark;
- use the large symbol below 48 px;
- place the logo on busy charts or photos without an Obsidian or white container.

## 7. Files
- Masters are generated by `build_kit.py` in this folder (`python3 docs/brand/build_kit.py` rewrites `svg/`). Change geometry there, never by hand-editing exports.
- In the web app, the header logo is `apps/web/src/components/brand/CryptoniteLogo.tsx`, built from the reversed small-cut symbol and the reversed wordmark.
- PNG exports, the presentation board and slides live outside the repo, in the brand kit folder (`brand/logo/final/`).
